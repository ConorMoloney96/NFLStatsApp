MODEL TRANSPARENCY

How it works

Every team starts at 1500 Elo. Ratings update after completed regular-season games based on opponent strength and score margin. Predictions use only earlier results, with a fixed 55-point home advantage.

P(home) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-(home Elo + 55 - away Elo)/400))

This is an experimental, uncalibrated Elo model. It does not account for injuries, lineups or weather.
